# 📊 Marketing & Sales Campaign Performance Analysis

## 📌 1. Project Overview & Objectives
This project analyzes marketing funnel efficiency by connecting top-of-funnel customer interactions (**Leads**) with closed sales records (**Deals**). The objective is to evaluate marketing channels (**Origins**), compute acquisition conversion rates, segment sales performance, and deliver actionable insights for ROI optimization.

## 🛠️ 3. Excel Concepts & Functions Applied
* **Data Integration & Lookup:** Connected `leads` and `deals` datasets seamlessly using `XLOOKUP`.
* **Custom Categorization:** Structured revenue distribution into deal categories using conditional multi-stage `Nested IF` formulas.
* **Data Aggregation & Metrics:** Calculated channel performance using `COUNTIFS`, `SUMIF`, and computed custom `Conversion Rate %` metrics.
* **Dynamic Visualization:** Built dynamic `Pivot Tables`, integrated customized conditional formatting for clean missing-data visualization, and deployed interactive `Slicers` & `Pivot Charts`.

---

## 🔑 4. Key Business Insights

* **Total Revenue Generated:** The campaign generated **EGP 61,784,006**, with **High-Value Deals** driving over **99.9%** (**EGP 61,755,000**) of total revenue.
* **Primary Growth Engine (Star Performer):** **`organic_search`** is the top-performing acquisition channel, generating **EGP 51,426,000** (~83% of total revenue) with a high conversion rate of **11.80%**.
* **Highest Conversion Efficiency:** **`paid_search`** recorded the highest lead-to-deal conversion rate at **12.30%**, generating **EGP 9,169,000**.
* **Low Efficiency Channels:** **`email`** (**3.04%** conversion rate) and **`direct_traffic`** (**8.45%** conversion rate) showed low conversion efficiency relative to captured lead volume.

---

## 🎯 5. Strategic Recommendations

1. **Scale Organic Content & SEO:** Continue investing heavily in SEO and content marketing, as Organic Search produces the vast majority of revenue with minimal incremental costs.
2. **Increase Paid Search Allocation:** Expand budget allocation for `paid_search` due to its top-tier conversion efficiency (12.30%).
3. **Optimize Email Campaigns:** Re-evaluate target criteria and email copywriting to improve low conversion rates.
4. **Prioritize High-Value Lead Funnels:** Align sales operations to immediately engage leads originating from Organic and Paid search channels to maximize high-ticket deal closures.
